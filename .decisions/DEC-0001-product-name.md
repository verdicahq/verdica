## Decision

The working name is **decisis**, from *stare decisis* — "to stand by things decided", the legal principle of precedent.

## Rationale

Short, pronounceable, available as a Python package name, and it states the product's job in two words of Latin.

## Rejected alternatives

- **adr-guard** — descriptive but generic; ties the product to one format name (ADR) when it accepts several.
- **decision-guardian** — accurate but long; reads as a category, not a name.
